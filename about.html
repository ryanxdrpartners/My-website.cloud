How a project runs.

Four stages, from the first conversation to a site that looks after itself.

  1. We talk

    A short call about your business, your customers and what the site has to do. You get a written quote afterwards.

  2. We design

    You see a working draft early. We change it with you until it is right.

  3. We launch

    We set up the domain, hosting and email, run our checks, and go live once you have approved it.

  4. We look after it

    From launch, the monthly plan covers hosting, updates and the changes you ask for.

What you can expect.

A site with a job

Every page exists to get someone to do something: call, book, buy or enquire. We start there, not with decoration.

The price up front

A written quote before any work starts, and one monthly plan after launch.

Everything stays yours

Your domain, your content and your site belong to you.

Questions people ask.

How long does a website take?

It depends on the size of the site. Your written quote includes a timeline, before any work begins.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain, your content and your site are yours.

What does the monthly plan include?

Managed cloud hosting, an SSL certificate, regular backups, uptime monitoring, security and software updates, and content changes and small improvements.

Can you rebuild my existing website?

Yes. We can redesign and rebuild a site that has fallen behind, and move your domain, hosting and email across for you.

What do I need to get started?

A conversation. Tell us about your business and what you want the site to do, and we will send a written quote and next steps.
