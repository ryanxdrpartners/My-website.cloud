Questions about our services.

What does website hosting include?

Managed cloud hosting with an SSL certificate, regular backups, uptime monitoring, and security and software updates, all handled by us.

Will my website show up on Google?

We build in the technical foundations: clean page structure, clear page titles and descriptions, fast loading times, a sitemap and Google Search Console set-up. Where you rank also depends on your content and your competition, so we do not promise a position.

Do you work with clients outside the UK?

Yes. We are based in the UK and work with clients anywhere in the world. Everything is handled online.

Can you move my domain and email?

Yes. We can register or transfer your domain, handle the DNS settings and set up mailboxes on your own domain, including moving existing email across.

Can you add booking forms or automate enquiries?

Yes. We set up enquiry and booking forms, email alerts for new enquiries, and simple automations that connect the tools you already use.
